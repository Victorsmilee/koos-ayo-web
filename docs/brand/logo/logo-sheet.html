KOOS-AYO — logo concepts

Concepts 1–6, then the two that were chosen. Each shown light, dark and in one colour, at favicon size and large.

The test that matters is the 16px column. Most logos die there. Anything that turns to mush at favicon size cannot be the primary mark, whatever it looks like big.

On option 5. The gap reads as "space to think" if you already know the product, and as "drifting apart" if you don't. For a relationship brand that is a real risk — shown here to judge, not recommended to lead with.
