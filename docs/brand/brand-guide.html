KOOS-AYO

Together, with joy. When a decision is genuinely balanced — a colour, a word, a layout — the name settles it. This is a togetherness product that takes conflict seriously, not a conflict tool with a friendly coat of paint.

Status. The product is pre-validation — MVP Brief v2 §17 gates it on survey evidence that does not exist yet. This guide documents what exists so it stays consistent, and no more. Everything in it is deliberately cheap to change.

The logo

The two O's of KOOS overlap — koos, together, and the lens is the space they share. The O of AYO carries a halo — ayo, joy. One lockup holding both halves of the name.

Colour

Crispness comes from structure, not colour. Three layers, each separated by both a fill change and a hairline. The palette itself stays quiet on purpose.

Voice

Most products can treat tone as taste. KOOS cannot — people open it while activated, sometimes mid-argument, sometimes frightened. A wrong sentence does real harm.

Say"It makes sense that you felt ignored."
Never"Your partner ignored you." — we do not know that.
Say"Sending that now might land as blame."
Never"That's a controlling thing to say." — character, not behaviour.
Say"Here are a few ways you could respond."
Never"You should apologise first." — orders, not options.
Say"Your partner may have experienced that as criticism."
Never"Your partner felt criticised." — a hypothesis stated as fact.
Say"This is beyond what I can help with."
Never"I'm so sorry, that must be incredibly hard 💙" — performed empathy.
